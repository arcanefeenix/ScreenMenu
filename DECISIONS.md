| D-36 | 2026-10-07 | **Pagination and page rotation (exact behavior).** (1) Layout: items are never split; a category bar is always followed by at least one of its items on the same page (otherwise the bar moves to the next page with its item); a category that continues repeats its bar with "(CONT.)"; hidden items and categories take no space; an item that cannot fit an empty page at the minimum sizes, or fit together with its bar, is reported and not drawn. A one-page menu does not rotate. The clock starts when output starts; leaving a test pattern restarts every menu at page 1. Rotation uses elapsed time (no drift), checked every 250 ms. (3) Content edits: if the number of pages is unchanged the page and the clock continue; if it changes, that screen restarts at page 1 so the new first page gets a full period. Any edit re-lays out immediately (a short pause of 250 ms merges bursts). (4) Test patterns replace menus and pause rotation. (5) Preview: it shows the same pages. **Follow** (default) mirrors the page on the LED if the menu is on output right now, otherwise it rotates on its own clock like the LED will. **Previous page / Next page** hold the preview on a page; this never changes the LED output, which keeps rotating. **Follow LED** resumes.
